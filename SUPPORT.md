# Support

Start with the [README](README.md), including its troubleshooting section,
and `lt --help`. For development setup, see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).

Search [existing issues](https://github.com/Swellshinider/LealTranscription/issues)
before opening a question, bug report, or feature request. Include:

- Windows, Python, and `uv` versions, and the installed project commit/version.
- The command, model, and device used; GPU and driver details for CUDA problems.
- Expected and actual behavior, with a minimal reproduction.
- Relevant errors with personal paths, credentials, and transcript content removed.

Use a synthetic or freely shareable recording only if audio is needed to
reproduce a problem. Do not upload private recordings or transcripts.
For vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead of opening an issue.
